ABOUT THIS MODEL

State of Health (SOH) is a percentage measure of a battery's current capacity relative to its original rated capacity. This project demonstrates how four measurable parameters — charge/discharge cycles, operating temperature, internal resistance, and charge rate — can be combined into a simple weighted formula to estimate SOH without needing a full lab teardown.

In a production BMS (Battery Management System), this kind of estimate is typically backed by regression models or neural networks trained on real charge/discharge datasets (e.g. NASA's battery aging dataset). This demo uses a transparent linear heuristic so the logic is easy to explain on a whiteboard.
